## Data profiling and plot purpose

The skill's `profile_data.py results/p6/cv_predictions.csv --group scenario` inspected all 8,640 OOF records: four scenarios each contain 2,160 events with no missing predictions or margins. Numeric center IDs, seeds and folds are identifiers/group metadata, not meaningful continuous covariates; the profiler's automatic numeric-ID typing/correlations are not interpreted scientifically or used as features. Margins and errors are strongly skewed. Signed margins cannot simply be log-transformed. All outliers and original event denominators are retained.

The selected scatter facets expose continuous prediction association and severe-margin compression. Alternatives such as hexbin or center-level points would respectively emphasize density or aggregation, but would obscure individual events/local patterns; transparent scatter retains all events. Scenario error densities show long tails rather than mean-only bars. The count heatmap diagnoses the fixed R=0 sign split. Decile oracle means with center-cluster bootstrap intervals diagnose continuous agreement, not a calibrated lifecycle threshold. No confirmatory significance test or star annotation is used.
